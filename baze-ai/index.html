Quick quiz

In Herzberg's two-factor theory, which of these is a hygiene factor?

B. Hygiene factors such as pay, working conditions, company policy and supervision stop people feeling dissatisfied, but they don't motivate. Motivators come from the work itself: achievement, recognition, responsibility and growth. Lecture 6, slide 9
